Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies. We keep the minimum needed to run the service and prevent abuse.

What we process

  • Your questions. Sent to an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI) to generate an answer. We don't store your questions or answers in our database. Don't include personal or sensitive information in questions.
  • Rate-limit counters. To enforce the daily question limit we store a counter keyed by a one-way SHA-256 hash of your IP address and the date. Counters expire automatically after about 2 days.
  • Standard request logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP address and user agent for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no profile, no selling or sharing of personal data, no advertising trackers.
  • The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and styles from the Tailwind CSS CDN, which receive your IP address when fetching those files.

Event data

Event listings come from public calendars of Houston venues and organizations. They describe events, not people.
